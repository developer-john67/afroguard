## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance enterprise interface engineered for autonomous agronomy, crop yield diagnostics, and cryptographic supply chain tracking. It harmonizes the physical tangibility of large-scale industrial agriculture with the exactitude of mission-critical AI telemetry.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Engineering** with **Organic Tactility** and restrained **Glassmorphic Depth**. Interfaces reject generic corporate sterility, instead projecting authoritative ecological stewardship through deep canopy darks, crystalline telemetry panels, and high-contrast botanical status indicators. 

Visual anchors prioritize clarity over decoration:
- Structural framing draws inspiration from architectural field grids, laboratory readouts, and topological maps.
- Diagnostic surfaces use disciplined, subtle frosted glass and low-key atmospheric glows to surface real-time sensor streams and spectral satellite imagery without visually fatiguing operators over long monitoring shifts.
- The interface projects confidence, empirical rigor, and bio-industrial vitality.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-density data analytics in the organic colors of canopy and soil, offset by precise diagnostic status emitters.

- **Primary (`#133E2B`):** Deep Forest Canopy. Anchor for primary buttons, structural navigational chrome, high-emphasis headers, and dominant active states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Dynamic Leaf Chlorophyll. High-vibrancy accent reserved for primary CTAs, real-time live sensors, optimal biometrics, and confirmed cryptographic proofs.
- **Tertiary (`#5D4037`):** Fertile Humus. Earth-tone anchor utilized for soil data overlays, geotechnical sensors, secondary status tags, and contextual environmental indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1C2420`):** Charcoal Pine. Deep carbon base infused with green undertones for deep body text, crisp data readouts, and primary structural borders.

### Surface and Canvas Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F9FBF8` (Crisp Warm Bone).
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with semi-translucent alpha variants (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) for frosted glass treatments.
- **Surface Level 2 (Nested Tables & Controls):** `#F3F6F2` (Warm Sand Mist).
- **Surface Inset/Muted:** `#E5EAE3` for structural divider lines, inactive progress tracks, and disabled states.

### Status & Verification Accents
- **Cryptographic Trust / Certified:** Emerald `#059669`
- **Warning / Soil Moisture Deficit:** Amber Ochre `#D97706`
- **Critical / Pathogen Detected:** Crimson Oxide `#DC2626`
- **Spectral / Telemetry Blue:** Cool Slate Cobalt `#475569`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy coordinates three distinct typefaces:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, sharp-edged industrial authority. Its geometric construction speaks to satellite tracking, spatial hardware, and automated yield machinery.
- **Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides exceptional neutrality, optical balance, and sustained legibility for continuous narrative reading, diagnostic reports, and configuration menus.
- **Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to cryptographic hashes, GPS coordinates, sensor readouts, payload signatures, and tabular metrics. The fixed-width rhythm prevents character jump during live stream updates.

### Usage Standards
- Enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` for all instances of metric display within tables and dashboard cards.
- Display and `headline-lg` levels must enforce tighter letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to retain density.
- Cryptographic keys and sensor coordinates must always be set in `data-tabular` or `data-caption` with uppercase transforms where applicable.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system geared for real-time operations centers and tablet-based field agronomy.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`gutter`) column gaps, 32px (`margin`) outer canvas margins. Supports persistent sensor sidebars, multi-spectral map canvas, and drill-down metrics panels simultaneously.
- **Tablet / Rugged Field Slate (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px gutter, 24px margins. Side rails collapse to an icon rail; diagnostic side-drawers transition to overlaid bottom sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, 12px (`gutter-mobile`) column gaps, 16px (`margin-mobile`) margins. Single-stream layout stack with sticky diagnostic trigger footers.

### Spatial Discipline
All component-level spacing operates on an 8px base rhythm (with a 4px half-step for dense data arrays and compact pill tags). Component interior padding must never visually crowd alphanumeric telemetry values; numbers must breathe against their containing card boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass panels, ambient botanical shadows, and precise structural keylines rather than thick opaque drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Flat `#F9FBF8` background canvas. Directly holds map viewports, topological contours, and primary section headers.
- **Card & Data Layer (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or translucent `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by a 1px keyline of `#E5EAE3` (or `rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.08)`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Level 2 - Hover / Active Panels):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`. Keyline transitions to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Flyouts (Level 3):** Frosted panels `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.06)`.

### Glassmorphism & Atmospheric Emissivity
Active tracking modes apply an emerald back-glow behind sensor status indicators (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.45)`). This signals verified connection health and active cryptographic consensus nodes.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural shape language (Soft `roundedness: 1`). Radii are kept compact to maximize screen utility in dense tabular readouts while evoking industrial, military-grade field tech:

- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons:** 0.25rem (`4px`) to preserve a crisp, technical profile.
- **Cards, Modals & Glass Containers (`rounded-lg`):** 0.5rem (`8px`).
- **Flyouts & Diagnostic Panels (`rounded-xl`):** 0.75rem (`12px`).
- **Telemetry Pills, Machine Badges & Micro Status:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) to create clear geometric contrast against rectangular data tables and viewports.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Forest Green (`#133E2B`) background with crisp white text, or Leaf Green (`#10B981`) background with dark charcoal pine (`#1C2420`) text for decisive actions. 0.25rem radius, uppercase label weight, hover elevation shift with a 1px glowing emerald perimeter.
- **Secondary / Technical Action:** Background `#F3F6F2`, border 1px solid `#E5EAE3`, text `#133E2B`. On hover, background shifts to `#FFFFFF` with border color `#133E2B`.
- **Destructive Action:** Low-tint crimson wash `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08)` border `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.3)`, text `#DC2626`.

### Diagnostic Chips & Verification Badges
- **Cryptographic Trust Pill:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`, text `#047857`, prefixed with a glowing 6px emerald status dot and a mono-spaced block height hash label.
- **Soil / Agronomic Tier Chip:** Earth-tone base `rgba(93, 64, 55, 0.08)`, border `rgba(93, 64, 55, 0.2)`, text `#5D4037`, label in `data-caption`.

### Data Tables & Sensor Lists
- Headers: `data-caption` uppercase, tracking `0.04em`, background `#F3F6F2`, text `#5D4037`, bottom border 1px solid `#E5EAE3`.
- Rows: Monospaced values right-aligned with fixed tabular layout (`tnum`). Zebra striping using alternating `#FFFFFF` and `rgba(243, 246, 242, 0.4)`. Hover state draws a left 2px green indicator bar.

### Inputs & Field Controllers
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#D1DCD0`, text `#1C2420`, 0.25rem radius.
- Active / Focus: Border `#10B981`, outline 2px solid `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`. Labels set in `label-sm` with persistent unit-of-measure indicators (`mg/kg`, `pH`, `NDVI`, `hPa`) anchored inside the right-hand container in `data-tabular`.

### Frosted AI Inference Cards
- Container constructed with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Header bar includes machine-readable confidence score (`e.g., 99.4% AI CONFIDENCE`) alongside a subtle gradient rule separating the diagnostic narrative from the sensor telemetry array.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (checkbox) or circular (radio), 16px dimension, 1px border `#8D6E63`.
- Checked state: `#133E2B` fill with an interior `#10B981` geometric glyph mark.
